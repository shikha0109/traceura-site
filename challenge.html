The Change Impact Challenge

Three questions decide every GxP change: what does it affect, how much testing does it need, and why? Test your judgement on realistic cases and get a personal score from an independent panel of experienced practitioners.

Free · About 2 hours · Open [opening date] to [closing date]

How it works

  1. Register below. We'll email you the case pack: 10 change cases at a fictional company, Quillmere Pharma.
  2. Pick any 3 cases. Each comes with a draft impact assessment written by a busy colleague. Some drafts miss real impacts. Some ask for far more testing than the change needs.
  3. Review the drafts as you would at work: what the change affects, what the draft gets wrong, and the validation scope you'd recommend.
  4. Get your results: a personal score report, the panel's full answer key and reasoning, and a certificate.

What you get

  • Independent feedback on your judgement, case by case, from an expert panel.
  • The panel's answer key: a practical lesson in risk-based, proportionate validation.
  • A participation certificate, and an optional named place in the results.
  • An invitation to the results webinar, and the results report before it's published.

Terms of participation

  1. The Change Impact Challenge is run by Traceura. Taking part is free and involves no purchase or obligation.
  2. All companies, systems, people and documents in the case pack are fictional. Any resemblance to a real organisation is coincidental.
  3. You must not use, copy, upload or refer to any document, data or information belonging to your employer or any other organisation. Entries must be your own work, based only on the case pack and your general knowledge.
  4. Entries are scored by an independent panel against an agreed answer key. The panel's scores are final.
  5. A human reviewer using Traceura's software also enters. Its answers and scores will be published in full.
  6. Results are published in anonymous, aggregated form. Your name is published only if you agree on your entry.
  7. Traceura may quote short, anonymous extracts from entries in the results report.
  8. Traceura may change or cancel the challenge if necessary, and will tell registered participants.

Privacy: we collect your name, work email, company and role to run the challenge, send you the case pack and your results, and invite you to the results webinar. We contact you about Traceura afterwards only if you tick the optional box. To have your details deleted, email founder@traceura.com. We don't sell or share your details outside the challenge team and judges.
