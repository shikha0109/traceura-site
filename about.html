Traceura / About

What is Traceura?

Traceura is decision intelligence and AI governance software for regulated life sciences. It helps GxP quality and computer system validation (CSV) teams assess change impact, right-size validation scope, find evidence gaps before review, and record the reasoning behind each decision.

Traceura (noun): decision intelligence for regulated enterprises. From traceability: making every regulated decision traceable to its evidence, its rules and the person accountable for it.

What does the name Traceura mean?

Traceura comes from traceability. The idea is simple: every important decision in a regulated company should be traceable to the evidence behind it, the rules that applied and the person accountable for it.

Is Traceura related to the parkour term traceur or the Traceur JavaScript compiler?

No. Traceura is a life-sciences software company. It is not connected to traceur, the term for a parkour practitioner, or to Google's former Traceur JavaScript compiler.

What is decision traceability?

Decision traceability means being able to show, for any quality or validation decision, what was decided, why, on what evidence, under which requirement, and who approved it, including whether anything has changed since.

Does Traceura's AI make decisions?

No. AI recommends and qualified people decide. Reviewers approve, edit or override every recommendation, and each recommendation shows the rule, requirement and source behind it.

Who is Traceura for?

Heads of CSV and validation, quality assurance, digital and IT quality, and quality systems teams at pharmaceutical, biotech, CDMO and medical-device manufacturers.
