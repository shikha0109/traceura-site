The problem

Your systems record what happened. Not why.

Quality and validation records capture what changed, when and who signed. The reasoning — why a requirement applied, why that validation scope was chosen, which evidence justified it — lives in experts' heads, spreadsheets and email threads.

So teams rebuild rationale for every audit, similar changes get assessed differently, and hard-won judgement walks out the door when people move on.

How it works

Retrieve. Reason. Recommend. Record.

A governed pipeline from raw documents to a defensible, signed decision.
